About Us

National Institute of Technology Hamirpur is one of the thirty-one NITs of the country, which came into existence on 7th August 1986 as Regional Engineering College, a joint and cooperative enterprise of the Govt. of India and Govt. of Himachal Pradesh. On 26th June 2002, REC Hamirpur was awarded the status of Deemed University and upgraded to National Institute of Technology. NIT Hamirpur is an institute of National importance set up by an act of Parliament namely the National Institute of Technology Act 2007 which received the accent of the President of India on 5th June, 2007. The provisions of Act came into force with effect from 15th August, 2007 as per notification S.O. 1384 (E) dated 9th August, 2007 of the Department of Higher Education, MHRD, New Delhi. As per provisions of the said Act, the Institute runs on non-profit basis.

Conference

National Conference on GST Rationalisation 2.0 (GROWTH-2026) is in collaboration with ICSSR. It addresses one of the most critical policy priorities for India today—building a modern, efficient and growth-driven GST framework. As India enters a new phase of economic expansion, GST 2.0 has become essential to remove structural gaps, reduce compliance complexities, and ensure that the tax system keeps pace with evolving business models and emerging sectors. This conference is important because GST reforms directly influence investment decisions, ease of doing business, competitiveness of Indian industries, and the ability of high-potential sectors—such as MSMEs, digital commerce, renewable energy, logistics, healthcare and agribusiness—to scale rapidly. A more rationalised GST framework can minimise cascading effects, encourage formalisation, strengthen supply chains and enable wealth creation across regions and socio-economic groups.

GROWTH-2026 provides an interdisciplinary platform for policymakers, researchers, industry leaders and practitioners to discuss evidence-based solutions for GST optimisation. Through expert lectures, research presentations and policy dialogues, the conference aims to:

  • Identify gaps and opportunities within the current GST regime
  • Propose simplified and sector-sensitive tax structures
  • Explore technology-enabled compliance reforms
  • Promote inclusive growth through improved tax equity and transparency

By bringing together academic insights and real-world perspectives, this conference seeks to guide the evolution of GST 2.0 as a catalyst for sustainable economic transformation. It reflects ICSSR’s commitment to fostering research-driven policymaking and supporting India’s journey toward a more robust, innovation-friendly and wealth-creating tax ecosystem
